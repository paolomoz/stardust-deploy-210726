The Brewery

Thirty winters in. The recipe is the room.

We started in a Heber Valley garage in 1996, brewing for a circle of friends who knew the range better than the resort map. The first batch was a stout dark enough to argue with February.

Three decades later, the lineup is six beers wide and the rule of the house is the same one we started with. The brewers write the copy. The mountain writes the schedule.

Nobody who works here is in a hurry, and the beer is better for it.

The founder

Started by a brewer. Still run by one.

The man in this room is the same one who brewed the first batch in 1996. He still walks the kettle every morning. He has opinions about every beer on this list and he has been overruled exactly once, by his daughter, on the matter of whether to add a hefeweizen to the lineup.

(She was right. Antelope has been on the menu since 2007.)
